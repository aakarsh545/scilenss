The planets and stars are illustrated test bodies. Displayed sizes are exaggerated markers. They follow relativistic trajectories around the central black hole; they do not attract one another. Physical collisions, spin dynamics, tidal disruption, comparable-mass mergers, and gravitational waves are not modeled.

The view shows coordinates on a PG time slice, not a ray-traced image. It omits lensing and light-travel delay. A probe stops when it crosses the horizon.

PG time is not a light-arrival clock. Probe proper time is integrated separately. Near unstable orbits, small numerical or launch differences can change the eventual outcome.
